A preset is a ready-made starting look for ATTAR. Each preset sets the colors, the fonts and the Arabic text style, and comes with its own home page layout. Every preset includes every feature and section; only the starting look differs.

ATTAR has four presets: **ATTAR**, **Sand**, **Majlis** and **Neon gulf**.

## ATTAR

Dark and warm: a near-black background, cream text and a gold accent, with a deep brown announcement bar. Headings are set in Cormorant, a fine serif, with a classic Naskh style for Arabic headings. This is the default preset.

**Home page:** Hero (**Smoke density** 75%), then the WhatsApp bar, Scent families, Featured products (4 products), Scent finder teaser, Payment and delivery, and Review highlight.

## Sand

Light and calm: a warm sand background, dark text and a deep green accent, with a green announcement bar. Headings are set in Cormorant in a medium weight, with Naskh Arabic headings.

**Home page:** Hero (**Smoke density** 40%), then Scent families, Featured products (8 products), Payment and delivery, Scent finder teaser, Review highlight, and the WhatsApp bar at the end.

## Majlis

Dark and rich: a warm brown background, cream text and a copper accent, with a red-brown announcement bar. Headings are set in Amiri, a classic serif, with Naskh Arabic headings.

**Home page:** Hero (**Smoke density** 90%), then Featured products (4 products), Scent finder teaser, Scent families, Review highlight, Payment and delivery, and the WhatsApp bar at the end.

## Neon gulf

Dark and modern: a deep navy background, cool white text and a bright mint accent, with a violet announcement bar. Headings are set in Rubik, a rounded sans serif, with modern Sans Arabic headings.

**Home page:** Hero (**Smoke density** 50%), then Featured products (8 products), Scent families, Scent finder teaser, Payment and delivery, and the WhatsApp bar. It has no Review highlight section; add one when you have reviews to show.

## Color schemes in each preset

Every preset comes with the same four color palettes: the dark gold palette, the light sand palette, the brown copper palette and the navy mint palette. The order changes so that each preset's own palette is **Scheme 1**, which is the **Page color scheme** in every preset.

| Scheme | ATTAR | Sand | Majlis | Neon gulf |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scheme 1 (page) | Dark gold | Light sand | Brown copper | Navy mint |
| Scheme 2 | Light sand | Dark gold | Dark gold | Dark gold |
| Scheme 3 | Brown copper | Brown copper | Light sand | Light sand |
| Scheme 4 | Navy mint | Navy mint | Navy mint | Brown copper |

In every preset, **Switch scheme for devices in light mode** is off, and **Light mode scheme** already points to the light sand palette (Scheme 2 in ATTAR, Scheme 3 in Majlis and Neon gulf). Turn the switch on to show it to visitors whose device is in light mode. Sand is already light, so the switch changes nothing there.

To edit a palette, go to Online Store › Themes › **Edit theme** › Theme settings › **Colors** and select the scheme. See [Theme settings]({{ site.baseurl }}/theme-settings/#colors).

## How to switch presets

1. Duplicate your theme first: in Online Store › **Themes**, select **…** next to ATTAR, then select **Duplicate**.
2. Go to Online Store › Themes › **Edit theme**.
3. Select the **Theme settings** icon in the sidebar, then select **Theme style**.
4. Choose a preset, select **Change style**, then select **Save**.

## What switching changes

Switching presets replaces the theme's design settings with the new preset's values:

- **Changes:** the color schemes and their colors, **Page color scheme**, **Light mode scheme** and **Switch scheme for devices in light mode**, the **Headings** and **Body** fonts, and **Arabic headings** and **Arabic body text**.
- **Reset to their defaults:** every other theme setting you choose from a list, a checkbox or a slider, including **Numeral system**, **Smoke**, **Note display**, **Campaign**, **Type scale**, **Page width**, **Page margin**, **Show WhatsApp**, **Widget zone**, **Selector style**, the payment badges and the switches in **SEO and sharing**. After switching, check Theme settings › **Typography**, **Layout**, **Numerals**, **Effects**, **Campaign**, **Floating widgets**, **Market selector**, **Payments** and **SEO and sharing**.
- **Stays the same:** your sections and their content, your home page layout, your menus, images such as the **Favicon**, and text you've typed in theme settings, such as social media links and the WhatsApp number.

Switching doesn't rearrange your home page. To use another preset's home page layout, reorder or add sections yourself using the lists above. You can also add ATTAR again from that preset's Theme Store listing; it's added as a separate theme in the **Draft themes** section, with that preset's home page.

If you don't like the result, leave the editor without saving, or publish the copy you duplicated in step 1.
