ATTAR is built Arabic-first. Arabic pages read right to left, English pages read left to right, and the theme's own text comes in natural Arabic and English. This page explains how to set up your languages and translate your content.

## Arabic and English out of the box

ATTAR includes complete Arabic and English translations of all the text the theme shows on its own: buttons, labels, messages and the default copy in sections. You don't need to translate any of it.

To sell in both languages:

1. Go to Settings › **Languages** and select **Add language**.
2. Add Arabic or English, whichever your store doesn't have yet.
3. Publish the language so customers can see it.

Your store's default language is the one customers see first. To change it, go to Settings › **Languages** and select **Change default**. Shopify gives each additional language its own web address, for example `/ar` or `/en`.

The theme editor labels are also translated. If your Shopify admin is set to Arabic, the theme editor shows ATTAR's settings in Arabic.

## Adding more languages

You can publish any language Shopify supports. Add it in Settings › **Languages**, then translate it with Shopify's free **Translate & Adapt** app.

The theme's built-in text is available in Arabic and English only. For any other language, translate the theme's text in Translate & Adapt as well as your own content. The app can suggest automatic translations that you then review.

## Automatic right-to-left layout

The layout follows the language of the page. You don't need to change any setting.

- **Right to left:** Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu.
- **Left to right:** English and all other languages.

On right-to-left pages, the whole layout mirrors: the logo and menu, the order of columns, carousels and sliders (they start from the right), arrows and the mobile menu. Photos aren't mirrored, so faces and bottle labels never appear reversed.

Settings that use **Start** and **End** follow the reading direction. In Arabic, start is the right side and end is the left side. For example, the **Photo position** of **Story image with text** and the **Widget zone** in Theme settings › **Floating widgets** both work this way.

Prices, SKUs, sizes and codes stay readable when Arabic and Latin text sit side by side, so a price such as "AED 250" never splits or flips.

## Arabic typography

Shopify's font library is the only source of fonts that Theme Store themes can use, and its fonts include Latin letters only. ATTAR therefore shows Arabic text in the Arabic fonts already installed on your visitor's phone or computer. These fonts are made for Arabic, load instantly and don't slow your store down.

Because they come from the device, Arabic text can look slightly different on an iPhone, an Android phone and a Windows computer. You choose the style, and each device uses its best matching font.

Go to Online Store › Themes › **Edit theme** › Theme settings › **Typography**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Headings** | The font for Latin headings, from Shopify's font library. |
| **Body** | The font for Latin body text, from Shopify's font library. |
| **Arabic headings** | The style of Arabic headings: **Naskh (classic)** or **Sans (modern)**. |
| **Arabic body text** | The style of Arabic body text: **Sans (modern)** or **Naskh (classic)**. |
| **Type scale** | Makes all text slightly smaller or larger, from 90% to 110%. |

**Naskh (classic)** is the traditional book style of Arabic, with strokes that vary in thickness. **Sans (modern)** is a clean style with even strokes, like the Arabic in phone interfaces. Attar, Sand and Majlis start with Naskh headings and Sans body text; Neon gulf uses Sans for both.

The Arabic style applies to Arabic text anywhere, including Arabic lines on English pages. On Arabic pages, the theme also adjusts text size and line spacing so Arabic reads comfortably.

## Numerals

Go to Theme settings › **Numerals** › **Numeral system**:

- **Western (0123)**: the default.
- **Arabic-Indic (٠١٢٣)**: the digits traditionally used with Arabic script.

The choice applies to the whole store, in every language. Prices, quantities, cart counts, sizes, ratings, dates, countdowns and timers all switch together, so a store never mixes the two systems. Decimal points and thousands separators in prices change to their Arabic forms too.

Numbers you type yourself, for example in a product description or a section's text, appear exactly as you type them. Write them in the system you chose.

Checkout and notification emails are run by Shopify, not by the theme, so they follow Shopify's own number formatting.

## Market and language selector

The market selector lets customers choose their country (and currency) and language. It appears only when your store has more than one country or more than one language. With **Language only**, it appears only when your store has more than one language.

1. Set up countries, currencies and the languages each market uses in Settings › **Markets**.
2. Choose what the selector shows in Theme settings › **Market selector** › **Selector style**:
   - **Flag, currency and language**
   - **Currency and language**
   - **Language only**
3. Turn the selector on or off in each place it appears:
   - **Header** section: **Show market selector**.
   - **Footer** section: **Show country and language selector**.
   - **Password** section: **Show market selector**.

Countries are listed with their currency, for example "United Arab Emirates (AED د.إ)", and each language is listed in its own script, such as العربية and English. The selector works as a simple form, so it opens no pop-up when the page loads.

## Translating your section text

Text you type in the theme editor is saved in one language. To show it in your other languages, translate it in Translate & Adapt:

1. Open the **Translate & Adapt** app and choose the language.
2. Under **Online Store**, select **Theme**.
3. Find the section by page (for example, the home page) or in the header and footer groups, and fill in each translation.
4. Save.

Translate your products, collections, pages, blog posts and menus in the same app.

Many fields help you avoid translation work:

- **Fields whose help text says "Leave blank to use the default…"** show the theme's built-in Arabic or English text when empty. Leave them blank unless you want your own words.
- **Ready-made copy options**, such as **Starter text** in **Story chapters**, **Ready-made copy** in **Scent finder** and **Ready-made caption** in **Lookbook**, fill empty fields with text that's already translated into Arabic and English. Anything you type replaces it.
- **The Page content option** in **Story text** shows the text of the page itself, which you translate with the page.

### Fields in the other script

ATTAR often pairs a heading with a short line in the other script: an English line on Arabic pages and an Arabic line on English pages. These fields have labels such as **Accent line**, **Eyebrow** or **Name in the other script**.

When you translate them, keep them in the opposite script. For example, if the **Accent line** shows عطور on English pages, its Arabic translation would be "Perfumes".

The logo and wordmark work the same way: in the **Header** section, **Logo for other languages** and **Latin wordmark** replace the Arabic logo and wordmark on pages that aren't in Arabic.

## What the theme's translations cover

ATTAR's Arabic and English translations cover all the storefront text the theme shows on its own:

- header, menus, search and search suggestions
- product pages, product cards, filters and sorting
- cart page, cart drawer and gifting
- customer account pages, the gift card page, the password page and the 404 page
- blog posts and comments
- the scent finder, compare, lookbook, boutiques and story pages
- labels that screen readers announce
- default copy and ready-made text in sections

Text you type in the editor, your products and your other store content are yours to translate. Checkout and notification emails are translated by Shopify.

You can change any built-in wording without editing code. Go to Online Store › **Themes**, select **…** next to ATTAR, then select **Edit default theme content**. Choose the language and search for the text you want to change.
