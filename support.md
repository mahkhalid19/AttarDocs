Send us your question with the form below. We reply to every request **within two business days**. You'll get an email confirming we've received it.

Before you write, the [FAQ]({{ site.baseurl }}/faq/) may already have the answer.

## Support form

<p><a class="btn" href="{{ site.support_form_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the support form</a></p>

The form opens in Google Forms in a new tab. It asks for your name, your store URL and a description of the problem, and you can attach up to five screenshots or PDFs. You'll need to sign in with a Google account to send it. That's how Google attaches your files and emails you a copy of your request.

## What to include

- Your store URL, and a link to the page with the problem.
- The language (Arabic or English) and the device and browser you used.
- A screenshot, if something looks wrong.
- If you've changed the theme code, tell us what you changed.

If we need to look at your store, we'll send you a collaborator request. To approve it, go to Settings › **Users**, filter by **Requests**, open our request and select **Accept request**. You can remove our access at any time.

## Support policy

**Covered:**

- Questions about theme settings, sections, blocks and presets.
- Help setting up features described in these docs, such as scent notes, gifting, numerals and campaigns.
- Bugs in the theme. We fix them in a theme update and tell you when it's available.

**Not covered:**

- Changes to theme code, or problems caused by code edits. See [Custom code and apps]({{ site.baseurl }}/custom-code/).
- Third-party apps, including how an app looks inside the theme. Contact the app's developer first.
- Shopify settings outside the theme, such as checkout, payments, shipping and domains. [Shopify Help Center](https://help.shopify.com) covers these.
- Store design, content writing and product photography.

For custom work, we suggest hiring a [Shopify Partner](https://www.shopify.com/partners/directory).

## Theme updates

Updates are free. When an update is out, a notice replaces the version number in Online Store › **Themes**. Select **Add to draft themes** to add an updated copy with your theme editor customizations carried over. Code edits are included when they don't conflict with the update; if they can't be included, the theme card says so. Save a copy of any code edits before updating. See the [changelog]({{ site.baseurl }}/changelog/) for what changed in each version.
