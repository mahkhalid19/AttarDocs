# ATTAR theme help

ATTAR is a Shopify theme for fragrance stores, built Arabic-first. Pages switch to right-to-left automatically in Arabic, prices and sizes stay readable in both directions, and all storefront text comes in natural Arabic and English. Fragrance details such as scent notes, sizes and concentrations have their own blocks, and gifting works on Shopify's own cart, with no app.

These pages explain how to set up the theme, what every setting does and how to get help.

## Before you start

- **Duplicate your theme before big changes.** In Online Store › Themes, select **…** › **Duplicate** next to ATTAR. You can then experiment on the copy and publish it when you're happy.
- **Preview in both languages.** Use the language selector at the top of the theme editor to check Arabic and English pages.
- **Keep your store data in Shopify.** ATTAR reads products, menus, metafields and policies from your admin, so most content changes happen there, not in the theme.
