When an update is out, a notice replaces the version number in Online Store › **Themes**. Select **Add to draft themes** to add an updated copy with your theme editor customizations carried over. Code edits are included when they don't conflict with the update; if they can't be included, the theme card says so. Save a copy of any code edits before updating.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Arabic-first layout with automatic right-to-left, Arabic and English storefront text, and a store-wide **Numeral system** setting.
- Four presets: Attar, Sand, Majlis and Neon gulf.
- Product pages with scent notes (note ribbon or classic notes list), color swatches, a gift option, local pickup, video, YouTube, Vimeo and 3D media, and a sticky add-to-bag bar.
- Collection pages with filters, sorting, quick add, express order and fragrance comparison.
- Cart drawer with gift wrap and message, notes, discounts and accelerated checkout.
- Seasonal campaigns for Ramadan, Eid, Saudi National Day and UAE National Day.
- Page templates for your story, boutiques, comparison, scent finder, lookbook, gifting and contact.
