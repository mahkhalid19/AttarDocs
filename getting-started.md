This guide takes you from adding the theme to a store that's ready for its first visitors. Each step is short and links to a page with the details.

## Add the theme

1. In the Shopify Theme Store, open the ATTAR listing for the preset you like best (Attar, Sand, Majlis or Neon gulf). Each preset has its own listing and its own home page layout.
2. Select **Try theme** to add it to the **Draft themes** section of Online Store › **Themes**, where you can preview and customize it.
3. Buy the theme when you're ready to publish it. Customers don't see it until you publish it.

ATTAR works on a new store with no products, menus or metafields. Empty sections show placeholders until you add your own content.

## Duplicate the theme before editing

Keep a copy you can return to. In Online Store › **Themes**, select **…** next to ATTAR, then select **Duplicate**. Make your changes on one copy and keep the other as a backup.

Duplicate again before any large change, such as switching presets, installing an app that adds code, or editing theme code.

## Pick a preset

A preset is a starting look: colors, fonts and the Arabic text style. ATTAR has four:

| Preset | Look |
|---|---|
| **Attar** | Dark, warm black with gold. The default. |
| **Sand** | Light, warm sand with deep green. |
| **Majlis** | Dark, warm brown with copper. |
| **Neon gulf** | Dark, deep navy with mint green. |

To change the look after you've added the theme, go to Online Store › Themes › **Edit theme** › Theme settings › **Theme style**. Switching replaces your colors and fonts, so do it before you fine-tune them. [Presets]({{ site.baseurl }}/presets/) explains what each one changes.

## First-day checklist

Open the theme editor from Online Store › Themes › **Edit theme**. Theme settings open from the **Theme settings** icon in the sidebar; the header, footer and page sections are listed in the sidebar itself.

### 1. Logo

Select the **Header** section and add your **Logo image**. Adjust **Logo width** until it sits well on both phone and desktop.

If your logo is in Arabic script, also add a **Logo for other languages** image, for example a Latin version of your brand name. It replaces the main logo on pages that aren't in Arabic. Without a logo image, the header shows the **Wordmark text** (your store name by default) and, on non-Arabic pages, the **Latin wordmark** if you fill it in.

The footer has its own **Wordmark** and **Latin wordmark**. See [Sections]({{ site.baseurl }}/sections/#header).

### 2. Menus

ATTAR uses the menus every Shopify store already has:

- **main-menu** for the header (**Desktop menu** and **Mobile menu**) and the first footer column (**First menu**).
- **footer** for the second footer column (**Second menu**).

Edit them in Online Store › **Navigation**. Nested menu items become columns in the desktop mega menu. Add your policy pages to the footer menu so customers can find them. See [Sections]({{ site.baseurl }}/sections/#header) for mega menu columns and tiles.

### 3. Colors and color schemes

Go to Theme settings › **Colors**. Each preset comes with four color schemes. **Page color scheme** sets the scheme for the whole store. Select a scheme to edit its colors.

To show a different scheme to visitors whose device is in light mode, turn on **Switch scheme for devices in light mode** and choose the **Light mode scheme**.

Keep text at a contrast ratio of 4.5:1 or higher against each background. See [Theme settings]({{ site.baseurl }}/theme-settings/#colors).

### 4. Typography

Go to Theme settings › **Typography**:

- **Headings** and **Body** are fonts from Shopify's font library. They apply to Latin text.
- **Arabic headings** and **Arabic body text** choose the style of Arabic text: **Naskh (classic)** or **Sans (modern)**.
- **Type scale** makes all text slightly smaller or larger.

Why Arabic uses system fonts: Theme Store themes can only use fonts from Shopify's font library, and the fonts in that library contain Latin letters only. Arabic text is shown in the Arabic fonts already installed on your visitor's phone or computer. These fonts load instantly and are designed for Arabic. See [Languages and right-to-left]({{ site.baseurl }}/languages-and-rtl/#arabic-typography).

### 5. Favicon

Go to Theme settings › **Theme** › **Favicon** and upload a square PNG of at least 180 × 180 px.

### 6. Social links

Go to Theme settings › **Social media** and paste the full address of each profile, for example `https://instagram.com/yourstore`. The footer shows an icon for each link when **Show social media icons** is on, and search engines use the links to connect your accounts to your store.

### 7. Announcement bar

Select the **Announcement bar** section at the top of the header. Type your message in **Text**, such as a delivery promise. Leave it blank to show a welcome message (or only the free-shipping line, if you set one), or the greeting of a seasonal campaign when one is running.

**Free-shipping threshold** adds "Free shipping on orders over [amount]" after your text. Enter the amount in your store currency; the line is shown only to shoppers paying in that currency. See [Sections]({{ site.baseurl }}/sections/#announcement-bar).

### 8. Home page sections

Open the home page in the editor and work from top to bottom. The default home page has:

- **Hero**: your main image or video, heading and buttons.
- **WhatsApp bar**: a WhatsApp contact strip. It uses the number from Theme settings › **Floating widgets**.
- **Scent families**: a tile for each scent family. Link each one to a collection.
- **Featured products**: pick a collection or hand-pick products.
- **Scent finder teaser**: set **Button link** to your scent finder page; the button shows only when it has a link.
- **Payment and delivery**: payment icons, cash on delivery, delivery and authenticity tiles.
- **Review highlight**: real customer reviews. Remove it until you have some.

Select **Add section** to add more, such as **Image with text**, **Slideshow** or **Email signup**. See [Sections]({{ site.baseurl }}/sections/).

### 9. Languages

ATTAR comes with Arabic and English storefront text. Add and publish languages in Settings › **Languages**. Arabic pages switch to right-to-left automatically. Translate your own section text with Shopify's Translate & Adapt app. See [Languages and right-to-left]({{ site.baseurl }}/languages-and-rtl/).

### 10. Markets

Set up the countries you sell to, their currencies and their languages in Settings › **Markets**. The header and footer show a country and language selector when your store has more than one country or language. Choose what it shows in Theme settings › **Market selector** › **Selector style**. With **Language only**, it shows only when your store has more than one language. See [Languages and right-to-left]({{ site.baseurl }}/languages-and-rtl/#market-and-language-selector).

### 11. Numerals

Go to Theme settings › **Numerals** › **Numeral system** and choose **Western (0123)** or **Arabic-Indic (٠١٢٣)**. Your choice applies to prices, counts, sizes and timers across the store, in every language. See [Languages and right-to-left]({{ site.baseurl }}/languages-and-rtl/#numerals).

### 12. Payment badges

Payment icons come from the payment methods turned on in Settings › **Payments**. To add regional text badges, go to Theme settings › **Payments** and turn on only the methods you offer: **mada**, **Tabby**, **Tamara** or **valU**. Each badge is hidden in markets where that method isn't available.

If you offer cash on delivery, list the country codes in **Cash-on-delivery markets**, for example `AE,SA`. See [Cart and checkout]({{ site.baseurl }}/cart-and-checkout/).

### 13. Policies

Write your refund, privacy, terms of service and shipping policies in Settings › **Policies**. ATTAR uses them in several places:

- The slim footer on product, cart, search, 404 and password pages links to your privacy policy and terms of service.
- Product page tabs about authenticity and shipping link to your refund and shipping policies until you write your own text.
- The full footer shows the links you add to your footer menus.

### 14. Preview and publish

Preview your store in Arabic and English, on a phone and on a computer. When you're ready, go to Online Store › **Themes**, select **…** next to ATTAR, then select **Publish**.
